After you start, msxp finds the EXP bar at the bottom of the game window. If it can't, open the preview and drag a box around the EXP numbers, then around the orange level badge.

Your data

History is saved in this browser only (IndexedDB) and kept for 90 days; totals stay with each character. Screen frames are read in memory and never saved.
