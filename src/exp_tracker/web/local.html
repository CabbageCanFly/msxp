About msxp /local

MapleStory Classic launched in October 2026 without a built-in EXP tracker, and Nexon doesn't offer an API for character stats. So msxp /local reads your own game window, right in your browser: the level and EXP bar, and the pickup messages for mesos and items.

  • Nothing to install. Share the game window in Chrome or Edge and press Start. Reading happens on this device.
  • Your data stays here. Readings, pickups and what the reader learns are kept in this browser only; nothing is sent to msxp.
  • EXP / hour, time to next level, mesos and items, with history and a free OBS overlay pop-out.

Questions or ideas? Message me on Discord, Twitch, Twitter, Instagram or GitHub. I'm @cabbagecanfly everywhere, so use whichever works for you.

    How do I add this to OBS?
    1. Pick your options above, then click Open pop-out. Keep that small window open (it can sit behind the game).
    2. In OBS, under Sources, click + → Window Capture → OK.
    3. Choose the window titled msxp overlay · On this device.
    4. Crop to the card (Alt+drag in OBS), or add a Chroma Key filter if you picked the green or magenta window background.

    A Browser Source can't show it: OBS uses its own browser, which can't see this browser's saved readings. The overlay shows progress only while this page is tracking; it never captures your screen itself.

    Your data

    Everything stays in this browser. Readings and pickups are kept in detail for 24 hours, then as one summary per day. Screen images are never saved. Clearing site data deletes it all.
